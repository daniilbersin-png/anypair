ABOUT ANYTHING

Launch a market around your idea.

Anything is a BNB Chain launchpad for asset-inspired tokens. Create a token around a stock, cryptocurrency, commodity or other priced reference, trade on its bonding curve, and graduate to PancakeSwap.

References, with context

Choose from stocks, crypto, commodity futures, published food surveys and priced game items. The catalogue only shows references with an available price, source and date. Unavailable or stale references cannot be selected for a new launch. References do not back tokens, set their trading price or provide redemption rights.

From curve to PancakeSwap

Loading current launchpad settings…

LP tokens go to the creator at graduation. They are not locked: the creator can withdraw liquidity. The launchpad owner can change curve settings and fees. The contracts are not audited.

Creator-funded rewards

A token creator can deposit USDT for a fixed-duration campaign. Holders deposit tokens to earn a proportional share over time and can withdraw their tokens at any time. Claims pay USDT. Product coupons show an estimated equivalent using a dated reference quote, with no physical redemption or price guarantee.

Find a token

Use its contract address. A token created here is not automatically listed on GMGN. Terminal visibility depends on the service’s supported markets and indexing.
