Activate Flap launches and USDT rewards

One deployment creates the Anything gateway to Flap and a new USDT reward vault. This transaction spends only BNB network fees. It does not launch a token or transfer USDT.

The gateway records each creator and product reference. New tokens trade through Flap. Existing tokens and rewards remain on their original contracts.

The gateway has no owner or upgrade mechanism. Flap is an external protocol with its own controls and fees. These contracts are tested but have not had an independent audit.
